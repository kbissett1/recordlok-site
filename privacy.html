Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

RecordLok is a mobile project-management application for construction and field-work projects. This Privacy Policy explains the information RecordLok may collect, how it is used, and the choices available to users.

Information We Collect

RecordLok may collect and process information that users provide while creating an account or using the service. This may include:

  • Name provided when creating or managing a RecordLok account.
  • Email address used for account authentication, project membership, support, and project activation.
  • User identifiers associated with RecordLok accounts and project access.
  • Photos and images that users choose to capture or upload for project documentation.
  • Project and user content, including project information, rooms, notes, inspections, problems, work updates, descriptions, contractor information, and other records entered into RecordLok.
  • Purchase and project-activation information, such as whether a project has been activated, the date of payment, transaction identifiers, payment provider, and amount associated with an activation.

How We Use Information

RecordLok uses collected information to:

  • create and maintain user accounts;
  • provide access to projects and project-management features;
  • store and display project records, photos, notes, inspections, and work activity;
  • manage project members, contractors, and assignments;
  • process and verify project activation;
  • respond to support, privacy, and account requests;
  • maintain the security, reliability, and operation of the service.

Supabase

RecordLok uses Supabase to provide services including authentication, database storage, file storage, and server-side application functionality.

Information associated with RecordLok accounts and projects may therefore be processed and stored using Supabase infrastructure as necessary to provide the service.

Payments and Stripe

RecordLok uses Stripe to process project-activation payments.

Payment-card information is entered directly into Stripe's checkout service. RecordLok does not directly receive or store complete payment-card numbers.

RecordLok may receive and retain information necessary to verify a successful purchase, including transaction identifiers, payment status, payment amount, payment date, and the RecordLok project associated with the transaction.

Photos and Camera Access

RecordLok may request access to a device camera or photo library when a user chooses to capture or select a project photo.

RecordLok does not access a user's photo library or camera for advertising purposes. Photos selected or captured for RecordLok may be stored as part of the user's project records.

Advertising and Tracking

RecordLok does not currently use collected RecordLok data for third-party advertising or cross-app advertising tracking.

RecordLok does not sell personal information as part of the ordinary operation of the service.

Information We Do Not Intentionally Collect

RecordLok does not currently request or intentionally collect precise GPS location, device contacts, microphone recordings, health information, or advertising identifiers as part of its normal functionality.

Service Providers

RecordLok may share information with service providers only as reasonably necessary to operate the service. Current service providers include infrastructure, authentication, database, storage, and payment providers such as Supabase and Stripe.

Those providers may process information under their own privacy and security practices.

Data Retention

RecordLok retains account and project information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain project records, meet legitimate operational requirements, resolve disputes, comply with applicable legal obligations, and process account-deletion requests.

Account Deletion and Privacy Choices

RecordLok users may initiate an account-deletion request from the Account & Privacy area inside the application.

Deletion requests are processed within 30 days, subject to information that may need to be retained for legal, security, fraud-prevention, transaction-record, or other legitimate operational requirements.

Users may also contact RecordLok Support regarding privacy questions or account-deletion requests.

Security

RecordLok uses reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information handled by the service. No electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Children's Privacy

RecordLok is intended as a project-management tool for work and field-project use and is not designed or directed toward children.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as RecordLok features, service providers, or data practices change. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will be revised when material changes are made.

Contact

For privacy questions, support requests, or account-deletion assistance, visit the RecordLok Support page or email sitemarksupport@gmail.com .

You may also review RecordLok's Terms of Service and Refund Policy.
