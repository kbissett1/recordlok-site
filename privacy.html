Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026

SiteMark provides mobile project-management tools for construction and field-work projects.

Information SiteMark may process

SiteMark may process account information, project information, project-member information, field records, photos or other project content that users choose to provide, and technical information required to operate the service.

Payments

Payments are processed by Stripe. SiteMark does not directly store complete payment-card numbers. Stripe processes payment information under its own privacy and security practices.

How information is used

Information is used to provide SiteMark functionality, maintain project access, process project activation, support users, secure the service, and improve reliability.

Sharing

SiteMark may use service providers necessary to operate the application, including infrastructure, authentication, database, and payment providers. Information is not sold as part of the ordinary operation of SiteMark.

Security

Reasonable technical safeguards are used to protect SiteMark information. No electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Contact

For privacy or support questions, visit the SiteMark Support page or email support@sitemarkapp.com .
